Hold a button or key to move. Release to send zero input. Tricks pause driving, then recover and enable movement automatically. STOP, posture buttons, tab switches and loss of window focus disarm; the next key press enables movement again.

Tricks

Every trick pauses driving, then automatically runs Recovery stand and Balance stand and enables WASD movement once it has finished. STOP or leaving this window cancels the sequence. Orange ones need at least 2 m of clear, flat space. Hind-leg stand and handstand are held for 6 s then ended; the End buttons end them sooner. The yellow line shows the robot's answer; a non-zero code means it refused.
