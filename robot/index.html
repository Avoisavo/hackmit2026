Hold a button or key to move at full joystick input (1.0). Release to send zero input. Driving recovers once; repeated drive keys reuse that recovery until a posture, trick or reconnection requires it again. Tricks pause driving, then recover and enable movement automatically. STOP, posture buttons, tab switches and loss of window focus disarm; the next key press enables movement again.

Tricks

Every action pauses driving and waits for Recovery stand before executing. After a trick finishes, it automatically runs Recovery stand and Balance stand and enables WASD movement again. STOP or leaving this window cancels the sequence. Orange ones need at least 2 m of clear, flat space. Hind-leg stand and handstand are held for 6 s then ended; the End buttons end them sooner. The yellow line shows the robot's answer; a non-zero code means it refused.
